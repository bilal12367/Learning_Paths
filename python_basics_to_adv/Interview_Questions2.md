## 🐼 **PANDAS INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**

### 🔰 **Basic Level**

1. What is Pandas? Why is it used?

3. How do you create a DataFrame from a dictionary?
4. What is the difference between `Series` and `DataFrame`?

10. How can you read a CSV file using Pandas?
11. What is the purpose of the `head()` and `tail()` functions?
12. How do you get summary statistics of a DataFrame?
13. How do you change the data type of a column?
14. How do you filter rows based on a condition?

15. Explain `groupby()` and provide an example.

18. How do you merge two DataFrames in Pandas?
19. What is the difference between `merge()`, `join()`, and `concat()`?
20. How do you handle missing values in Pandas?
21. What is the use of `pivot_table()`?
22. How do you deal with duplicated data in a DataFrame?
23. What is a MultiIndex in Pandas?
24. Explain the difference between `sort_values()` and `sort_index()`.
25. What are the differences between `.values`, `.to_numpy()`, and `.as_matrix()`?
26. How do you create a new column based on the values of other columns?

### 🧠 **Advanced Level**

27. What is chaining in Pandas, and why should it be avoided?
28. What’s the difference between `agg()` and `transform()` in groupby?
29. How does Pandas handle large datasets, and what are some optimization techniques?

37. What is the use of the `query()` function?
